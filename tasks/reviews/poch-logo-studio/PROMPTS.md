# POCH 1441 Logo Studio - Prompt Set

## Tool mode

Built-in image generation, one separate generation per symbol. The images are concept studies, not production vector artwork.

## Shared base prompt

Create one square, text-free logo symbol for POCH 1441. Timeless 2026 system-mark clarity without imitating an existing brand. Flat, vector-like, optically precise, with one small human irregularity. Deep graphite and broken white only. Generous clear space. No words, letters, numbers, card suits, gold, luxury styling, mockup, perspective, shadows, gradients, bevels, material textures, heraldry, seal, casino language, watch branding or spirits branding. The mark must remain legible at 24 px and be drawable from memory after five seconds.

The supplied comparison board was used only as a structure reference. The material board was not used as a style reference.

- `01-brett.png`: Poch board topology reduced to eight outer basins, one ring and a dominant center.
- `02-schlag.png`: A controlled physical knock deforms a heavy ring and displaces its inner core.
- `03-o-wird-c.png`: A closed ring becomes an open form through one precisely removed fragment.
- `04-rhythmus.png`: Three tactile beats with different visual weights, avoiding barcode, Wi-Fi and audio-wave clichés.
- `05-achse.png`: Abstract mirrored architecture inspired by the structure of 1441, without readable digits.
- `06-reihe.png`: Five bodies form a sequence with one decisive gap and one moving element.
- `07-drei-akte.png`: Three strong arcs nearly complete one round while remaining individually legible.
- `08-versetzte-mitte.png`: A cropped ring, displaced core and short connector create controlled imbalance.
- `09-meridian.png`: A heavy ring with one deliberate seam and a slightly off-center point.
- `10-bruch.png`: Two massive planes create a narrow negative diamond gap without startup or fintech styling.
- `11-topographie.png`: A few organic contours gather around a quiet center and survive small scale.
- `12-geste.png`: One human table gesture translated into an abstract mark, without handwriting or letters.

## Jury rule

Material, color and animation may only be explored after the monochrome silhouette passes the 24 px test.

## Shortlist variation set

Each shortlist variation was generated with a separate built-in image-generation call. Every prompt retained the shared monochrome constraints and used the relevant baseline image only as a structural reference.

### Das Brett

- `brett-a-acht-mulden.png`: clarify all eight outer basins while reducing rotor associations.
- `brett-b-offener-tisch.png`: introduce one strategic opening into the round.
- `brett-c-drei-phasen.png`: organize eight positions into three visibly weighted phase groups.
- `brett-d-menschliche-runde.png`: apply subtle optical irregularity and a minimally displaced core.

### Der Schlag

- `schlag-a-druckstelle.png`: one precise indentation records the contact point.

### O wird C

- `oc-a-fragment.png`: retain one exact heavy fragment just outside the opening.
- `oc-b-keil.png`: use an asymmetric optically organic cut instead of a technical gap.
- `oc-c-doppelzustand.png`: combine closed and open states in one compact abstract form.
- `oc-d-klopfoeffnung.png`: make the physical cause of the opening visible at the edge.

### Die Reihe

- `reihe-a-luecke.png`: one missing position controls the rhythm without becoming a barcode.
