# POCH 1441 brand system

## Current direction: Knock Ring

`Knock Ring` is the active digital and physical production candidate. Its
heavy open ring is the abstract Poch board; the controlled notch is the moment
of the knock. Three small enamel inlays carry the three phases without turning
the silhouette into a diagram. The coral diamond remains the stake core.

The wordmark uses a dedicated optical-size O: the same Knock Ring silhouette,
but without the three micro-inlays, with a shallower notch and a larger coral
core. This keeps `POCH` readable at 19-26 pt instead of shrinking the App Icon
into the header. The App Icon and large symbol retain the three phase inlays.

Source masters:

- `poch-knock-glyph-v6.svg` - full-color symbol for digital use
- `poch-knock-glyph-v6-mono.svg` - one-color production symbol
- `poch-knock-wordmark-v6.svg` - primary 1180 x 220 color lockup
- `poch-knock-wordmark-v6-mono.svg` - one-color 1180 x 220 lockup
- `poch-knock-app-icon-v6.svg` - square iOS source master

Formal trademark clearance remains a separate product gate. Until that gate is
green, the mark is approved for internal builds and physical prototypes only.

## Rejected direction: rounded-square Knock Groove O

The former rounded-square `Knock Groove O` is not approved for production. Its
counter and groove collided visually with the existing otelo wordmark. The new
elliptical ring does not reuse that silhouette: it is circular, materially
heavier and paired with the three phase inlays.

The reusable idea is the small diamond as the stake core. New directions must
derive their silhouette from POCH's board, three phases or physical knock - not
from a notched rounded-square O.

## Manufacturing requirements for the successor

- Use the monochrome SVG masters as the source of truth.
- Minimum physical symbol size: 8 mm.
- At 8-15 mm, use `poch-knock-glyph-v6-mono.svg` without color inlays. The
  cut-outs remain materially wider than 1.2 mm at the 8 mm minimum.
- At 17 mm and above, the regular geometry may be milled, laser marked,
  embossed, debossed or screen printed without optical effects.
- Do not add bevels, metallic gradients, shields, seals or ornamental rings to
  the core mark. Material belongs to the product surface, not the logo geometry.
- `1441` is a quiet provenance mark. It must never outweigh `POCH`.

## Color system

- Ink blue `#0B0E14` is the primary digital ground and matches the app.
- Warm ivory `#F4F0E8` carries `POCH` and the primary symbol.
- Muted brass `#C5A059` is reserved for the provenance `1441` and phase one.
- Copper coral `#C96E55` marks the small stake core.
- Deep emerald `#1A5E4E` and amethyst `#4A2E65` carry phases three and two.
- The production mark must always remain reproducible in one color.

The app icon uses the Knock Ring geometry and remains recognizable at 24 px.
iOS applies its own icon mask; the raster master therefore remains square and
unmasked.
